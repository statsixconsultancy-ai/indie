HERO VIDEO SLOT
---------------
Drop your own, licensed video here and name it:  hero.mp4
(H.264, ~1920x1080, a loop of 10-20 seconds, under ~10 MB works best.)

If assets/hero.mp4 exists, the home page plays it as the hero background.
If it does not exist, the site shows its built-in live cinematic scene.

Only use footage you own or have a licence for.
